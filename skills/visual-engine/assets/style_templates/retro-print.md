# Style: retro-print

## Visual Fingerprint
Risograph or screenprint aesthetic. Limited inks (typically 2-3), visible registration offset, halftone dots, slight ink texture, slight imperfection in alignment. Feels like a 1970s zine or a contemporary risograph print shop output.

## Reference Phrasing for Gemini
Lead the prompt with one of:

- "Risograph print aesthetic, two-color print with visible registration offset, halftone dots, slight ink texture."
- "1970s screenprint poster style, limited ink palette, bold flat shapes with halftone shading."
- "Contemporary riso print illustration, three-ink layers with intentional misalignment, organic ink texture."

## Subject Treatment
- Bold flat shapes work best. No rendering or smooth gradients.
- Texture comes from halftone dots, not from painting.
- Slight misregistration is the signature: "blue layer offset 2px from the orange layer."
- Subjects can be playful, illustrative, even cartoonish.

## Composition Notes
Pairs well with: `pattern-repetition`, `centered-subject`, `split-frame`, `overhead-flat-lay`.

Avoid `cinematic` compositions like `frame-within-frame` or `silhouette` — too photographic for the print aesthetic.

## Palette Pairings
Best with: `burnt-poster`, `tropical-ink`, `terminal-green`.
Strict rule: 2-3 inks plus paper. Specify: "Two inks only — [color 1] and [color 2] — printed on cream paper background."

## Things to Avoid in Prompts
- "Photorealistic," "3D," "rendered" — all wrong
- "Smooth gradient" — riso doesn't do gradients, it does halftone dots
- More than 3 colors — breaks the print constraint

## Example Prompt Skeleton

```
Risograph print aesthetic, [N]-ink print with visible registration offset and halftone dots. [SUBJECT: bold flat illustration of specific subject — e.g. "a stack of books leaning slightly, with a coffee cup on top"]. [COMPOSITION]. Inks: [color 1] and [color 2] (and [color 3] if 3-ink), printed on cream paper background. Halftone dot texture in the shaded areas. Slight registration offset (2-4 pixels) between ink layers. 4:3 aspect ratio, organic print imperfection. No smooth gradients, no photorealism, no more than [N] colors.
```

## Best For
- Listicles ("10 books that…", "5 lessons from…")
- Retrospectives and year-in-review posts
- Posts about books, music, culture
- Anything with a slight nostalgic or playful tone
