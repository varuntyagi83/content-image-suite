# Style: hand-drawn

## Visual Fingerprint
Loose ink lines with watercolor or gouache washes. Visible brushstrokes, paper grain showing through. Personal, intimate, sketchbook quality. Feels like a friend drew it, not an agency.

## Reference Phrasing for Gemini
Lead the prompt with one of:

- "Hand-drawn ink illustration with watercolor washes, loose confident line work, visible paper grain."
- "Sketchbook-style illustration, ink and gouache, in the tradition of Maira Kalman or Christoph Niemann."
- "Editorial sketch with ink lines and color washes, slightly imperfect, intimate aesthetic."

## Subject Treatment
- Subjects are often single figures, single objects, or small intimate scenes.
- Imperfection is part of the style — name it: "loose lines, occasional ink blots, washes bleeding slightly outside the lines."
- Avoid overly detailed rendering. The charm is in suggestion.
- **Face treatment depends on the subject's role:**
  - For a **named protagonist**, the face should be drawn with simple but recognizable features — eyes, expression, hair shape. Loose lines are fine; the figure should still feel like a specific person, not a placeholder.
  - For a **generic figure** or a background figure, simplification or face-omission is appropriate. The Niemann/Kalman tradition often draws figures with minimal facial detail; that's intentional and works.

## Composition Notes
Pairs well with: `centered-subject`, `negative-space-dominant`, `rule-of-thirds-left/right`.

Avoid `pattern-repetition` (looks too systematic for hand-drawn) and `worms-eye-view` (too dramatic).

## Palette Pairings
Best with: `paper-and-pencil`, `sunwashed`, `bone-and-rust`.
Hand-drawn works best with limited palettes — 3 colors plus paper white. Specify: "Three watercolor washes only: [color 1], [color 2], [color 3], on cream paper."

## Things to Avoid in Prompts
- "Photorealistic" — total contradiction
- "3D" or "vector" — total contradiction
- "Highly detailed" — undermines the sketchy charm
- Saturated neon colors — the watercolor aesthetic wants muted tones

## Example Prompt Skeleton

```
Hand-drawn ink illustration with watercolor washes, loose confident line work, visible paper grain. [SUBJECT: single figure or small intimate scene — e.g. "a woman in her 60s standing at a kitchen window holding a mug of tea, looking out at a garden"]. [COMPOSITION]. Watercolor washes in: [3 colors with hex codes] on cream paper background. Loose lines, occasional ink blot, slight bleed of color outside lines. 4:3 aspect ratio, sketchbook quality, intimate mood. No photorealism, no digital aesthetic.
```

## Best For
- Personal essays
- Beginner guides ("how I learned to…")
- Storytelling posts
- Posts about learning, growth, change
- Reflective or meditative content
