# Style: collage

## Visual Fingerprint
Mixed-media cut-paper aesthetic. Layered textures, visible paper edges, halftone dots, vintage magazine clippings combined with hand-drawn marks. Imperfect, deliberately rough, with a sense of human assembly.

## Reference Phrasing for Gemini
Lead the prompt with one of:

- "Mixed-media collage illustration, cut paper layered with halftone dots, vintage magazine clippings combined with bold gestural marks."
- "Dada-inspired collage aesthetic, scissor-cut paper edges visible, layered textures, surrealist juxtaposition."
- "Contemporary editorial collage, photographic elements combined with painted shapes and hand-cut paper."

## Subject Treatment
- Subjects can be assembled from incongruous parts: a person with a pixelated head, a building with a hand for a door.
- Photographic fragments combined with illustrated elements work well.
- Texture is critical — name the textures: "newsprint texture, halftone dots, torn paper edges, ink smudges."
- Slight rotation and offset of elements gives the hand-assembled feel.

## Composition Notes
Pairs well with: `centered-subject`, `split-frame`, `pattern-repetition`, `frame-within-frame`.

Avoid `rule-of-thirds` compositions — they imply a clean photographic eye, contradicting the assembled feel.

## Palette Pairings
Best with: `burnt-poster`, `tropical-ink`, `sunwashed`, `paper-and-pencil`.
Collage tolerates more colors than other styles because the layering naturally separates them.

## Things to Avoid in Prompts
- "Smooth," "polished," "clean" — all contradict the style
- "Vector" or "digital" — collage should look physical
- Symmetrical compositions — too neat for the aesthetic

## Example Prompt Skeleton

```
Mixed-media collage illustration, cut paper layered with halftone dots and vintage magazine fragments. [SUBJECT: assembled from incongruous parts — e.g. "a businessman whose head is a vintage television displaying static, his suit cut from old newspaper print, standing on a paper-cut city skyline"]. [COMPOSITION]. Visible textures: torn paper edges, halftone dots, faded newsprint, ink smudges. Palette: [PALETTE with hex codes]. Slight imperfection in alignment, hand-assembled aesthetic. 16:9 aspect ratio, contemporary editorial collage quality. No clean digital aesthetic.
```

## Best For
- Cultural commentary and satire
- Posts about media, attention, modern life
- Strong opinions and provocations
- Posts where the visual itself should make a statement
