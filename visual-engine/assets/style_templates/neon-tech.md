# Style: neon-tech

## Visual Fingerprint
Synthwave / cyberpunk aesthetic. Dark backgrounds, neon glow, magenta and cyan dominant, occasional grid lines or circuit patterns. Feels like the inside of a server room or a 1980s sci-fi film, modernized.

## Reference Phrasing for Gemini
Lead the prompt with one of:

- "Cyberpunk neon-tech illustration, dark background with magenta and cyan glow, retro-futuristic aesthetic."
- "Synthwave digital illustration, neon outlines on deep navy background, 1980s sci-fi film aesthetic modernized."
- "Vaporwave-influenced tech illustration, neon glow effects, grid floor in distant perspective."

## Subject Treatment
- Tech objects: servers, monitors, headsets, network nodes — all native.
- Human figures should be silhouetted or rendered as outlines with neon edges.
- Glow effects are core to the style: name them explicitly ("magenta glow rim light," "cyan ambient light").
- Backgrounds often contain a grid or wireframe element.

## Composition Notes
Pairs well with: `centered-subject` (with strong glow makes a single object the focal point), `silhouette`, `worms-eye-view` (looking up at glowing tech), `diagonal-motion`.

Avoid `overhead-flat-lay` and `birds-eye-view` — neon-tech is about looking *at* something monumental, not down at it.

## Palette Pairings
Best with: `midnight-circuit`, `terminal-green`, `electric-dusk`.
Always specify the dark background color first: "Deep navy background (#0A1428), neon accents in magenta (#FF1F8F) and cyan (#00E0FF)."

## Things to Avoid in Prompts
- "Bright daylight" or "soft natural light" — this is a night/interior style
- "Hand-drawn" or "painterly" — this is digital and clean
- "Pastel" colors — neon-tech needs saturated, glowing colors
- Cliché AI imagery: glowing brains, blue circuit lines forming a face, robot hands. These ruin the style.

## Example Prompt Skeleton

```
Cyberpunk neon-tech illustration, dark background with magenta and cyan glow. [SUBJECT: specific tech object or scene — e.g. "a rack of servers viewed from below, cables running in glowing arcs to an unseen ceiling"]. [COMPOSITION]. Lighting: deep ambient darkness with [SPECIFIC GLOW DESCRIPTION — e.g. "magenta rim light from below, cyan accent from a single monitor"]. Background includes a faint perspective grid receding to a vanishing point. Palette: [PALETTE with hex codes, specifying dark background]. 16:9 aspect ratio, retro-futuristic atmosphere, high contrast. No daylight, no clichéd glowing brain or circuit-face imagery.
```

## Best For
- AI, machine learning, future-of-X posts
- Crypto / web3 (sparingly — overused in this space)
- Cybersecurity
- Posts about systems, infrastructure, or networks
- Sci-fi adjacent essays
