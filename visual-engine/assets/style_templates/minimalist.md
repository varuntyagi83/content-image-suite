# Style: minimalist

## Visual Fingerprint
A single subject (or very few) on a vast empty background. Geometric, restrained, confident in its silence. Heavy use of negative space. The visual says less so the message says more.

## Reference Phrasing for Gemini
Lead the prompt with one of:

- "Minimalist illustration, single subject in vast negative space, geometric and restrained."
- "Swiss design influenced minimalist composition, one focal subject, expansive empty background."
- "Architectural minimalism, geometric forms, negative space dominant, calm and confident."

## Subject Treatment
- One subject is typical. Two at most.
- The subject should have clean geometric form — a sphere, a simple object, a single figure in profile.
- Resist adding details. The charm comes from restraint.
- If the subject is a person, render simply: a silhouette, a back-view, or a heavily abstracted figure.

## Composition Notes
Pairs naturally with: `negative-space-dominant`, `centered-subject`, `silhouette`, `rule-of-thirds-left/right`.

Avoid `pattern-repetition`, `collage`-like layering, or busy compositions.

## Palette Pairings
Best with: `cold-architecture`, `monochrome-noir`, `soft-laboratory`.
Minimalism wants 2-3 colors maximum. State this: "Only two colors: [color 1] for the subject, [color 2] for the background."

## Things to Avoid in Prompts
- "Detailed," "intricate," "ornate" — all contradict the style
- "Multiple elements" — minimalism resists multiplication
- Texture-heavy descriptions — surfaces should be flat or smooth gradient

## Example Prompt Skeleton

```
Minimalist illustration, single subject in vast negative space. [SUBJECT: one specific simple object or figure, geometric and restrained — e.g. "a single brass key floating in empty space" or "a lone figure in a long coat seen from behind, walking away"]. [COMPOSITION]. Background is a flat field of [BACKGROUND COLOR]. Subject rendered in [SUBJECT COLOR] only. Two colors total. Soft directional shadow, otherwise flat. 16:9 aspect ratio, contemplative empty quality. No additional elements, no decoration, no texture beyond gentle gradient.
```

## Best For
- Philosophy and meditation posts
- Productivity and focus content
- Single-thesis essays
- Posts where less is the message
