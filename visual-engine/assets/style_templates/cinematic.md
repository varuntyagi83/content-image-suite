# Style: cinematic

## Visual Fingerprint
Photorealistic frames that look like stills from a feature film. Shallow depth of field, dramatic lighting, considered camera angles. The image feels like a moment captured rather than constructed.

## Reference Phrasing for Gemini
Lead the prompt with one of:

- "Cinematic film still, shot on 35mm, shallow depth of field, dramatic lighting, in the style of contemporary character drama."
- "Photorealistic cinematic frame, anamorphic lens, color graded for warmth, mood reminiscent of A24 films."
- "Documentary photography aesthetic, natural light, candid composition, photojournalistic."

## Subject Treatment
- Real-looking people in real-looking situations.
- Hands and faces will sometimes be imperfect; choose compositions that hide hands or show them at rest.
- Environmental details matter: textures, props, wear.
- Subjects should be doing something specific, not posing.
- **Face treatment depends on the subject's role:**
  - For a **named protagonist** (the post's author, a profiled person, "I" in a personal essay), show a clear face at three-quarter or front angle with a readable expression. Cinematic style supports this naturally — it's the "close-up" or "medium shot" of film.
  - For a **generic figure**, silhouette, back-of-head, or shallow-focus blur all work and are cinematic. The face being out of focus is a deliberate cinematic choice, not a defect.
  - If unsure, default to **three-quarter angle with shallow focus on the face** — keeps the figure feeling like a specific person while preserving the cinematic look.

## Composition Notes
Pairs well with: `rule-of-thirds-left/right`, `frame-within-frame`, `silhouette`, `worms-eye-view`, `birds-eye-view`.

Avoid `pattern-repetition` and `overhead-flat-lay` — these contradict the cinematic feel.

## Palette Pairings
Best with: `monochrome-noir`, `velvet-financial`, `electric-dusk`, `bone-and-rust`.
Cinematic frames need a unifying color grade. State the grade explicitly: "color graded warm" or "color graded cool teal-and-orange."

## Lighting Direction
Cinematic style demands a specific lighting choice. Pick one:

- "Golden hour from the left, warm rim light"
- "Cool blue overcast daylight, soft shadows"
- "Single hard practical light source from a window, deep shadows on the opposite side"
- "Magic hour, sky at twilight, ambient cool light"
- "Harsh fluorescent overhead office light, sterile mood"

## Things to Avoid in Prompts
- "Illustration" or "cartoon" (contradicts the style)
- Overly clean compositions — cinematic frames have texture and imperfection
- Smiling subjects looking at camera (looks like stock photo)

## Example Prompt Skeleton

```
Cinematic film still, shot on 35mm with anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field. [SUBJECT: specific person, specific action, specific environment]. [COMPOSITION]. [LIGHTING DIRECTION]. Color graded [warm/cool/teal-orange/neutral] in the palette of [PALETTE NAME]. 16:9 cinematic aspect ratio, photorealistic, professional film production quality. No stock photo aesthetics.
```

## Best For
- Personal essays and narratives
- Long-form deep dives
- Posts about people, places, or moments
- Anywhere mood matters more than message
